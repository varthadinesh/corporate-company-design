What happens when the grid goes down.

Run a quick simulation to see the changeover a Kiran GenForce installation makes the moment mains power fails.

  1. 01

    Mains fails

    Grid supply drops. Without backup, the site goes dark.

  2. 02

    Panel senses

    The auto-mains-failure panel detects the loss and signals the engine.

  3. 03

    Genset starts

    The Powerol engine cranks, builds speed and stabilises voltage and frequency.

  4. 04

    Load transferred

    Power flows to the building again. The lights stay on.
